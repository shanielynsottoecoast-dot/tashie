About Call of Duty

Call of Duty is one of the most iconic first-person shooter franchises in gaming history. Since its debut in 2003, the series has defined what it means to step into the boots of a soldier — from the beaches of Normandy to modern battlefields and beyond.

Every installment blends cinematic campaigns, fast-paced multiplayer, and unforgettable moments with squadmates. Whether you're clearing a building with your team, holding a hardpoint in overtime, or surviving endless rounds of the undead — every match tells a story.

For millions of players around the world, it's more than a game. It's a mission.
